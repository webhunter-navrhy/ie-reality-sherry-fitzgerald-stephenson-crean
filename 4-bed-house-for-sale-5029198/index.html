Description

We are delighted to bring to the market this spacious family 4 bed residence, ideally situated in the mature and well-established residential development of Racecourse Lawn, Tralee.

The property offers generous and well-proportioned accommodation throughout, including a welcoming entrance hall, two reception rooms, a fitted kitchen and dining area, and an additional study / office on the ground floor also together with spacious bedroom accommodation on the first floor.

While the property would benefit from some modernisation, it has been well maintained and offers an excellent opportunity for the successful purchaser to update and personalise the home to their own taste.

Externally, the property enjoys a west facing mature and private rear garden with a patio area and a rear conservatory, providing an attractive outdoor space for gardening, entertaining and family enjoyment.

This home also benefits from being directly opposite the large green amenity space situated in Racecourse Lawn.

Racecourse Lawn is a popular residential location within easy reach of Tralee Town Centre and a wide range of local amenities including schools, shops, sporting facilities and public transport links. The location provides all the benefits of an established residential setting while remaining convenient to the town centre and surrounding road network.

With its generous accommodation, mature garden and excellent location, this property presents an ideal opportunity for families and first-time buyers seeking a home with plenty of potential in a highly convenient area of Tralee.
